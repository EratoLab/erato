## Summary

Under a component kit that supplies ChatTopLeftAccessory, seating the accessory in flow ( 1091) left three rules from the share feature assuming the conversation starts at the page top: the share button floated at the top right, the delegated-run strip reserved its corner, and the message list padded 56px for the floating button. With the accessory above the layout shell that band was empty, and the "Beginning of conversation" marker and the share button sat a full row below the picker.
