## Summary

A wait child is awaited in-process by the turn that dispatched it, and that turn is what writes the answer into the slot it reserved. When that turn is gone, nothing does. rearm delivery after child decision does not cover it either — that hook re-arms a result delivery row, and only an async child owns one.
